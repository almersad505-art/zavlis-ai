AI for Students

Use AI to support understanding, not replace it. Follow your institution’s rules and verify sources.

NotebookLM

Studying and researching a defined collection of sources.

Start with one reliable source. Ask a specific question, open its reference in the original file, and verify it before adding more material.

Summaries can misread a source. Review citations and keep your files up to date.

ChatGPT

Everyday drafts and ideas refined through conversation.

Draft a short message with a clear audience, length and tone. Check names, numbers and sources before using it.

Can make factual mistakes. Tool limits vary. Check sources before relying on an answer.

Organizing ideas and preparing a draft while checking sources.
