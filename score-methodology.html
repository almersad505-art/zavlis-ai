Zavlis AI Score

A transparent 100-point framework. No final number without complete evidence.

CriterionWeightRequired evidence
Quality25A defined task, saved output and accuracy criteria.
Free Value20A task achievable for free, documented limits and an appropriate license.
Ease of Use15Observed setup steps, time and friction.
Features15Documented features suited to the task and plan.
Arabic Support10Official availability and an actual Arabic text, layout or pronunciation test.
Privacy / Trust10Data policies, permissions and deletion controls; a policy alone does not prove safety.
Value5A documented price and a defined task with measurable benefit.

Calculation and publication

Each criterion is assessed from 0 to 5 against published task-specific requirements: 0 is a demonstrated failure; 1 mostly unmet; 2 limited value with clear friction; 3 meets the task with limitations; 4 strong results with few issues; 5 meets all declared test criteria. Criterion points = assessment ÷ 5 × weight. Publish a total only when every criterion has evidence. Missing evidence remains pending, not zero.
