Editorial Policy

Evidence, limits and independence.

Sources and dates

We prioritize official documentation. Tool reviews show the review date and distinguish a documented feature from a recommendation. We do not invent prices, user counts, ratings or limits.

Tests and scores

Source-based reviews are not described as hands-on benchmarks. A final score requires evidence for every weighted criterion. Unverified evidence remains pending.

Comparisons

Recommendations are task-specific and limited to the tools we actually review. Complementary workflows are not presented as identical replacements. “Best” means a practical starting point within this collection, not proven market superiority.

Affiliate relationships

Affiliate links are disclosed and do not control scores or editorial conclusions. Official terms remain the final source for purchases and licensing.

Corrections

Information is reviewed when a factual error or a source change is identified. No unverified contact channel is presented as active.
