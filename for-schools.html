For Schools and Universities

Zavlis AI is an independent, free Saudi project that helps students and teachers choose AI tools by the task they need to do, with clear limits and links to official sources.

What it offers your institution

A free starting point you can share with students: they pick a task (summarizing a source, writing an email, preparing a presentation, transcribing audio) and see suitable tools, how to start, and what to check before relying on the result.

No account, no subscription and no paid plan.

Responsible use first

We encourage students to follow their institution’s rules, verify sources and use AI to support understanding, not replace it.
