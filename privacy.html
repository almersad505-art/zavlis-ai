Privacy

How this static website handles information.

Scope

Reviewed 11 October 2026. This site has no account registration, payment forms or message submission. Its application code does not collect names, email addresses or payment data.

Search and filters

Tool searches and filters run locally in your browser. This code does not send the search to a server or save it to a database.

Language preference

Your language choice is kept in sessionStorage in your browser for this browsing session. It contains only the language code, not your searches or personal details. Browser storage restrictions may prevent this preference from being remembered.

Hosting and cookies

Microsoft Clarity (project yytrv421ps) loads only after analytics consent on this GitHub Pages site. It can collect interaction, device and technical request data. Tool inputs/results and quick-form content are masked for session recordings. Hosting can process technical request data for operation and security under its own policies. We cannot verify its retention periods or promise that hosting does no processing.

External and affiliate links

External services apply their own privacy policies. The ElevenLabs affiliate provider may use referral tracking or cookies after you follow its link. We do not control that processing.

Changes

We do not sell visitor data. Analytics preference is saved in localStorage. Rejecting prevents this site from loading Clarity; withdrawing consent reloads without it. Previously set provider cookies are not manually removed. Review Microsoft privacy terms for their retention and processing.
